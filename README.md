## What is a perfect number?
In number theory, a perfect number is a positive integer that is equal to the sum of its positive proper divisors, that is, divisors excluding the number itself.
[1] For instance, 6 has proper divisors 1, 2, and 3, and 1 + 2 + 3 = 6, so 6 is a perfect number. 
The next perfect number is 28, because 28 has proper divisors 1, 2, 4 , 7, 14, and 1 + 2 + 4 + 7 + 14 = 28.

source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_number)
